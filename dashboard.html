Quick help

Frequently asked questions

Is this my live PayPal data?

The dashboard shows an illustrative sample workspace. Only actions run through the PayPal Sandbox use sandbox data, and nothing here moves real money.

What can NovaPay AI see?

NovaPay AI answers only from the activity shown in this workspace and cites the records behind each answer. It makes suggestions only and never takes an action on its own.

How do approvals work?

Open Approvals to review each proposal and its evidence. A PayPal Sandbox action needs a second confirmation with the reference and amount before it runs; unsupported actions are simulated only.

Where are my settings stored?

Preferences are saved in this browser and apply immediately. Use Data & privacy to export your information or reset every preference to its default.

How do I change my password or sign out?

Go to Security. You can update your password, set an automatic sign-out time, or end the session on this device.
